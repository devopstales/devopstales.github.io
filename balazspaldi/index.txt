## About

I help engineering organisations move from ad-hoc infrastructure work to operable cloud-native platforms: clear ownership, automated delivery, observable production, and security controls teams will actually use.

As a DevOps Architect at [Shiwaforce](https://www.shiwaforce.com/), I work with enterprise customers across banking, telecom, and pharma — assessing maturity, designing target operating models, implementing platforms, and enabling teams through training. I collaborate daily with developers, architects, and consulting colleagues: translating constraints into pipelines, infrastructure-as-code, and runbooks rather than handing over a cluster and walking away.

I publish hands-on guides on [DevOpsTales](https://devopstales.github.io/) covering Kubernetes networking, GitOps, policy, and platform engineering.
